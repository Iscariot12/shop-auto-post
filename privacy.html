Privacy Policy

Shop Auto Post

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Overview

Shop Auto Post is a personal automation application developed for creating and publishing product-related content on Pinterest.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when using the application.

2. Information We Process

The application may process information necessary to provide its functionality, including information associated with the developer's Pinterest account and information required to access the Pinterest API.

Product information obtained from supported shopping and affiliate services may also be processed for the purpose of generating product-related content.

3. Pinterest API

Shop Auto Post uses the Pinterest API to create and manage Pins and Boards on the developer's own Pinterest account.

Pinterest authentication is performed through Pinterest's authorization mechanisms. The application does not collect or store the user's Pinterest password.

4. Purpose of Data Processing

Information is processed only for purposes related to operating the application, including:

  • Creating product-related content
  • Generating AI-assisted titles and descriptions
  • Creating and managing Pinterest Pins
  • Managing Pinterest Boards
  • Scheduling content publication

5. Data Sharing

Personal information is not sold or rented to third parties.

Information may be transmitted to external services only when necessary to provide the application's intended functionality, such as Pinterest or other services explicitly used by the application.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the application and provide its intended functionality.

7. Data Deletion

The developer may delete application-related data when it is no longer required.

Requests concerning deletion of application-related data may be made by contacting the developer.

8. Third-Party Services

Shop Auto Post may use third-party services, including Pinterest and affiliate shopping services, to provide its functionality.

Each third-party service may have its own privacy policy and terms of service.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data processing practices change.

The latest version will be published on this page.
