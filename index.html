Shop Auto Post

Shop Auto Post is a personal application that helps generate product-related content and publish it to social media platforms.

Purpose

This application uses AI-assisted processing to create product introduction content based on products available from online shops and publishes Pins to the user's authorized Pinterest account.

Supported Services

The application may support multiple online shopping services and social media platforms. The supported services may be expanded in the future.

Privacy

This application is intended for personal use. We do not intentionally collect or sell personal information through this website.
